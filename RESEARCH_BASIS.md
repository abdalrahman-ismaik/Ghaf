# Ghaf Research and Content Basis

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-01

**Purpose:** Evidence and culturally grounded content for Feature 003 Revision 2

**Product status:** Research basis and design guardrails; not implementation evidence, medical,
legal, psychological, financial, or religious advice

This document translates available evidence into cautious product rules. It does not establish that
a League or monetary reward will benefit every Child, authorize diagnosis, promise lasting behavior
change, issue a religious ruling, or replace review by UAE parents, children, educators,
accessibility and child-safety specialists, Arabic speakers, qualified counsel, or religious
advisers.

In this document, capitalized **Parent** means the authorized parent-or-guardian product role, not
only a biological parent.

## Revision and Evidence Boundary

Revision 2 approves separately accessed Parent and Child experiences, a normalized invite-only
Friendly League, optional private Family Rewards, age-adapted bounded Coach presentation, and the
Alexandria/Readex typography system. This changes the 2026-08-28 ten-route product baseline.

The earlier automated and web-proxy results remain historical evidence for the superseded build
only. They do not validate the revised access model, pairing, reauthentication, Leaderboard,
Family Reward, simulated voice controls, typography, navigation, Android behavior, accessibility,
or human comprehension. Revision 2 design and implementation validation are on hold until approved
Stitch frames arrive and the active Spec Kit artifacts are updated.

## Research Conclusions

1. Use tasks and rewards to scaffold a specific routine, not to purchase obedience, affection,
   faith, disclosure, eating, or basic needs.
2. Make goals positive, observable, achievable, age/ability appropriate, and agreed before work.
3. Give specific human praise first; keep Seeds predictable, permanent, and separate from money and
   League position.
4. Support autonomy, competence, and relatedness through choices, attainable steps, permitted help,
   renegotiation, opt-out/rest, and a cooperative family result.
5. Competition may increase engagement in some educational contexts, but its advantage over
   noncompetitive designs is not consistently robust and does not establish benefit in family
   routines.
6. If competition is used, normalize opportunity rather than difficulty or volume: five nominated
   Challenge Leaves, a maximum weekly score of 100, shared ties, and no speed tiebreaker.
7. Monetary Family Rewards are an optional Parent-controlled promise, not an evidence-proven default,
   a wallet, a universal Seed exchange, or a prize for defeating another Child.
8. Never remove earned progress, shame a miss, condition an essential need, or make a virtual tree
   die.
9. Apply `visibilityScope`, `leagueEligible`, and `circleEligible` before every shared visual or
   counter. These are separate decisions with different disclosures.
10. Use bounded AI only for the current Parent-approved task. Do not create a companion, therapist,
    religious authority, emotion detector, truth judge, or diagnostic system.
11. Treat the UAE living landscape as a product metaphor and separate symbolic growth from measured
    environmental impact.
12. Use synthetic access, profiles, League data, rewards, media, and voice simulation for SMAC;
    real child data or money requires separately approved production work.

These are interface defaults, not developmental judgments. A Parent must be able to shorten a task,
turn off audio, remove evidence, allow extra time, specify assistive support, choose “completed with
help,” change future League Leaves, and pause League or future rewards without reducing dignity or
deleting earned progress.

## Permanent Seeds and Garden Guardrails

- Eligible acquisition-task awards remain fixed at 4, 6, 8, 12, or 15 Seeds; no random multiplier.
- The Child sees the fixed Seed award or “Parent recognition, no Seeds” before accepting.
- An accepted task earns its displayed award when completed with permitted help; only a smaller
  task agreed before acceptance may display a smaller award.
- Parent confirmation is idempotent; duplicate taps never create duplicate Seeds, growth, Leaves,
  canopy updates, or Family Reward progress.
- A retry does not remove existing Seeds, show a failure badge, or change a past reward plan.
- A missed day creates no debt or broken-streak punishment. Trees never wither because of illness,
  travel, disability, access, rest, or League opt-out.
- `standard`/`fade-first` acquisition may earn the displayed award and persistent eligible growth.
  Maintenance earns neither. Only recurrent `fade-first` acquisition prompts a Parent review after
  three confirmations. `recognition-only` uses `not_applicable` and never creates Seeds, persistent
  growth, a Challenge Leaf, or a circle event. No phase transition is automatic.
- No purchasable currency, loot box, ad, scarcity countdown, loss mechanic, or
  engagement-maximizing nudge is allowed.

## Friendly League Research Interpretation and Guardrails

The peer-competition evidence is mixed and indirect. Revision 2 therefore treats League as an
optional family experience to be tested, not as a clinically or educationally validated intervention.

- Each participating Child starts the week with exactly five Parent-nominated, age/ability-appropriate
  Challenge Leaves. `Weekly Growth Score = confirmed Challenge Leaves ÷ 5 × 100`; the maximum is 100.
- Help and accessibility adaptations receive full credit. Extra tasks may grow the garden but do
  not buy rank. Ties share position; speed and evidence are not tiebreakers.
- Permanent personal Seeds/garden and the cooperative family canopy remain visible alongside rank.
- Prayer, affection, emotional disclosure, relationship closeness, food consumption, private
  wellbeing, hygiene, disability-related routines, and recognition-only activities are ineligible.
- Participants see nickname, tree avatar, position, score, and completed Leaves only. They never see
  another Child's task, evidence, age, accommodations, Seed total, reason for rest, or reward.
- Child assent, Parent control, a no-penalty rest week, pause, and opt-out are required. Opting out
  must not hide the garden, remove Seeds, reduce access, or create a public explanation.
- Prepared bilingual reactions must be few, warm, and non-evaluative. They carry no count, popularity
  metric, negative reaction, sarcasm, pile-on, reply thread, or free text. Families can mute them.

### League review triggers

Pause the League and review the design or family setting when a Child repeatedly wants to hide the
screen, shows distress about position, avoids a safe task because it will not score, attempts unsafe
extra work, loses sleep, experiences teasing or family conflict, feels pressured to disclose private
information, cannot access equivalent opportunities, or focuses on rank at the expense of personal
meaning. The product must not infer these states from behavior, face, or voice; the trigger comes
from a Child, Parent, or named human reviewer.

## Family Reward Research Interpretation and Guardrails

AAP and CDC guidance supports clear goals, meaningful agreed rewards, praise, avoiding demerits,
and adjusting or phasing out a program. It does not establish a universal Seed-to-money rate or
prove that money improves long-term routines for Ghaf users. Revision 2's monetary option is a
Parent choice governed by stronger protections:

- Family Reward is private, optional, non-custodial, and independent of League position. Types may
  be money, experience, privilege, or gift; Seeds and garden work without it.
- The plan specifies one Child, a prospective eligible personal milestone, and a Parent-chosen
  reward before progress begins. The Parent sees the maximum amount promised that month and
  reauthenticates before a monetary promise.
- States are `Promised → Unlocked → Given`. An unlocked promise cannot be removed as punishment;
  agreed terms cannot be changed retroactively. Future plans may be paused or revised prospectively.
- Praise and garden growth appear before money. There is no wallet, cash balance, transfer, custody,
  universal exchange rate, wager, paid boost, or winner-take-all prize.
- Money never conditions food, water, clothing, safe shelter, sleep, healthcare, education,
  transport, ordinary family contact, affection, safety, or ordinary religious participation.
- Money is prohibited for prayer, affection, emotional disclosure, eating, body/health outcomes,
  relationship closeness, proof of love, and private wellbeing or disability-related routines.
- Apply eligibility at the contributing task/version before progress. Unknown or prohibited
  confirmations add zero Family Reward progress; a landscape milestone cannot bypass this policy
  by relying on aggregate displayed growth.
- Prefer the least controlling effective reward, preserve choice and meaning, and review whether
  the Child would prefer a nonmonetary plan, different goal, or no external reward.

### Family Reward review triggers

Pause future plans and review when the Child reports pressure, bargaining displaces task meaning,
the Parent cannot comfortably afford the maximum promise, siblings receive materially unequal
access without a family-understood reason, the Child avoids unpriced prosocial activity, a reward
becomes expected indefinitely, a target is repeatedly unreachable, the family disputes the agreed
terms, or the reward begins to function as punishment or leverage. Existing earned Seeds, growth,
and unlocked rewards remain intact.

## Competition and UAE Foundation

Khalifa University describes SMAC 2026 as a functional mobile-app competition using AI for
sustainability under **“AI Adventures in Sustainability.”** Its published objectives mention mobile
development, AI thinking, sustainability solutions, and live demonstrations; no more detailed
judging rubric is published, so one must not be invented. [SMAC 2026](https://www.ku.ac.ae/smac)

The UAE Ministry of Family frames its work around cohesive and resilient families rooted in
Emirati values. Family connection is therefore a UAE-relevant product co-benefit. Ghaf's SMAC
sustainability claim rests on sourced environmental behaviors, not on treating every family action
as environmental impact. [UAE Ministry of Family](https://family.gov.ae/en/)

Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi identifies Ghaf as the UAE national tree and a historic and cultural
symbol of stability and peace; it separately describes its ecological importance in desert
biodiversity. EAD also identifies Ghaf, Samar, and Sidr as native trees in its tree-tagging program.
[Ghaf tree](https://www.ead.gov.ae/en/discover-our-biodiversity/plants/ghaf-tree),
[native tree-tagging](https://www.ead.gov.ae/en/Media-Centre/News/Tree-Tagging-Programme-2024)

The meanings the Ghaf product assigns to the other landscape species—responsibility, learning,
reflection, or heritage—are original product storytelling, not official UAE symbolism. They
require a short disclosure and local review.

## Curated Starter Task Catalog

All titles use neutral Modern Standard Arabic noun phrases so they work for Salem and Alya. Emirati
dialect, gender variants, and religious content require local review. `A` means an adult is present
or participating; `G` means guardian approval/check. `visibilityScope` is `child_guardian` or
`household`; `circleEligible` is a separate Boolean that may be true only for Green Impact with
`visibilityScope = household`. A circle projection contains one coarse family Green Impact action
and never the task record, Child identity, or Seeds.

`leagueEligible` is a third, independent weekly nomination. The catalog does not make a task a
Challenge Leaf automatically. Before each week, the Parent selects exactly five suitable tasks per
participating Child. Recognition-only tasks and the prohibited prayer, affection, emotional
disclosure, eating, private wellbeing, hygiene, and disability-related categories are never
eligible. A confirmed League Leaf exposes only one completed opportunity; it never changes
`visibilityScope`, publishes the catalog row, or makes a non-Green task `circleEligible`.

Catalog phase defaults are explicit: every row with a numeric Seed award starts in `acquisition`;
every recognition-only row uses `not_applicable`; the untracked prompt has no phase. `standard` is
reserved for finite/one-time work. A recurrent routine must use `fade-first`, and its third confirmed
completion prompts—but never forces—the Parent's future-phase decision.

## Arabic, English, Voice, and Typography Basis

- Modern Standard Arabic is the safe default for task requirements, safety, privacy, Family Reward
  terms, and sensitive content. A light Parent-approved Emirati/Gulf register may be considered for
  greetings and encouragement only after review by Emirati parents, children, and language
  specialists.
- A prepared phrase library may include reviewed Arabic-English code-switch examples. P0 must not
  generalize from those fixtures to a claim that it understands natural code-switching or arbitrary
  speech.
- Arabic and English are fully localized modes, not two languages repeated on every ordinary
  control. Arabic starts in true page-level RTL; English uses matched LTR. Directional navigation
  mirrors, but tree avatars, checkmarks, and other nondirectional symbols do not.
- Alexandria is the required display family for titles, garden names, and milestone moments.
  Readex Pro is the required family for body copy, controls, tasks, AI dialogue, League data,
  settings, and Family Reward details in both languages.
- Arabic uses generous line height, medium/readable weights, no artificial letter spacing, no
  clipped diacritics, and explicit mixed-direction handling for strings such as `AED 25`,
  `١٢٠ بذرة`, dates, and names. Rankings, Seeds, and reward amounts require tabular numerals.
- Font scaling must preserve at least 48dp controls, long Arabic labels, reading order, and the
  distinct Parent and Child hierarchies. These properties require frame review, Android rendering,
  and named Arabic/accessibility review; choosing the font files alone is not validation.

## AI and Child-Safety Basis

UNICEF's 2025 guidance calls for age-appropriate child AI, clear disclosure, privacy and data agency,
protection from manipulation and dependency, human support, and safety/accountability.
[UNICEF Guidance on AI and Children 3.0](https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/media/11991/file/UNICEF-Innocenti-Guidance-on-AI-and-Children-3-2025.pdf)

The APA advisory recommends protective defaults, reduced persuasive design, interaction limits,
human support, strong image/voice safeguards, and clear warnings that AI is not professional health
advice.
[APA AI and Adolescent Well-being Advisory](https://www.apa.org/topics/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning/health-advisory-ai-adolescent-well-being.pdf)

[OpenAI's Under 18 API Guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-checks/under-18-api-guidance)
requires additional safeguards for minors, including age-appropriate disclosures and content,
monitoring/reporting and escalation mechanisms, and heightened care with data. It states that
personal data of children under 13 or the applicable digital-consent age should not be processed by
OpenAI services without zero data retention. A production implementation must re-check the current
official guidance rather than rely on this summary.

### Child Coach Invariants

- State that it is AI and can be wrong.
- Restrict the conversation to the current Parent-approved task.
- Ages 6–8 use a slower prepared voice, one very short instruction at a time, curated intents
  without free text, and an early Ask an adult action.
- Ages 9–11 use two or three steps, friendly explanations, quick-choice responses, and structured
  intents/template input.
- Ages 12–14 use respectful concise language that is never babyish, with guardian-enabled bounded
  text or simulated push-to-talk. No band gets unrestricted chat.
- Never invite secrets, exclusivity, attachment, dependence, or endless conversation.
- Never infer emotions from a face or voice, recognize a face or voice, diagnose, score personality,
  judge truthfulness, or judge religious sincerity.
- The P0 push-to-talk experience is a prepared synthetic audio/transcript simulation, not capture,
  speech recognition, or live Child media processing. The target interaction still shows a
  deliberate press, visible recording state, transcript, delete-before-send, replay, captions,
  slower playback, and a clear send action so the future boundary can be evaluated honestly.
- Never suggest ambient, continuous, or background listening.
- Make photo/voice optional and tell the Child what the Parent can see before sending.
- For the MVP, use prepared synthetic media and deterministic responses as the always-available
  fallback. Only the Parent task-refinement path may make a real model call with synthetic input,
  through an approved secure server boundary, structured validation, and honest live/prepared
  labels. The Child Coach remains prepared in P0.
- Escalate ordinary task hazards to an adult. A future high-risk safeguarding path must support an
  alternate trusted adult and be reviewed before real deployment.

### Parent Guide Report Shape

1. **Window and facts:** observable completion/reminder data and time period.
2. **Strengths first:** specific actions or improvements.
3. **Pattern:** neutral change, explicitly uncertain and never labeled normal/abnormal.
4. **Question:** one open question the Parent can ask the Child.
5. **Adjustment:** smaller step, cue, choice, or environmental support.
6. **Outside help:** only when appropriate; suggest a pediatrician, school professional, or another
   qualified person without making a diagnosis.

Example: “During the last seven days, Salem completed two recycling tasks independently and asked
for help with the evening routine three times. Ask which step feels unclear. Consider showing only
one step after dinner this week.”

Prohibited outputs include lazy, defiant, abnormal, good/bad child, ADHD, anxiety, personality
scores, deception claims, emotion scores, parenting-quality judgments, and religious-worth scores.

## Privacy and Legal Review Triggers

The UAE's child digital-safety and personal-data frameworks are relevant to a real deployment.
The prototype must not claim compliance. Before any real child accounts, social sharing, or media
processing, obtain current UAE legal review and complete a child-rights/privacy impact assessment.

- [UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 26 of 2025 on Child Digital Safety](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/3912)
- [UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download)

Minimum production questions include guardian consent and Child assent, age assurance, privacy by
default, minimum collection, separate media consent, task-scoped retention/deletion, no provider
training or secondary use, no biometric templates or face/voice identification, no emotion
inference, Parent visibility, trusted-adult escalation, League/circle access, Child participation
controls, Family Reward records, financial/consumer disclosures, reporting, withdrawal, and provider
data handling.
