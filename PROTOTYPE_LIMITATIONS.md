# Prototype Limitations

## Capability Truth

Ghaf — غاف is an MVP prototype for Khalifa University SMAC 2026. Feature 003 Revision 3 planning
inherits Revision 2's separately accessed Parent and Child experiences, fair invite-only Family
League, optional private Family Rewards, age-adapted bounded coaching, and bilingual typography,
then proposes a private Growth Journey with an Impact Path, badges, and sourced learning. It is not
production-ready and must not be presented as an authenticated child service, secure family
network, banking product, diagnostic system, religious authority, verified impact platform, or
legally compliant deployment.

As of 2026-09-03, R001 Welcome and first-time Parent onboarding are implemented with scoped
automated/web evidence and Android build/install evidence. Every later Revision 2 surface and all
Growth Journey runtime/design validation remain on hold until their approved Google Stitch frames
arrive and the active Feature 003 artifacts are reconciled.

## Superseded Historical Baseline

The 2026-08-28 deterministic ten-route build and its automated and bilingual web-proxy results
remain historical evidence for that exact implementation. It used `/role`, forced role switching,
a cooperative `/circle` screen, no Family Reward, and the previous typography. Those results do not
carry forward to any Revision 2 criterion.

The old build does not validate separate sign-in, Child PIN/picture access, pairing, biometric or
passkey presentation, Parent reauthentication, League score/privacy, reward-plan states, voice
controls, Alexandria/Readex rendering, revised navigation, physical Android, or named human review.
Feature 002 evidence remains historical as well and must not be rewritten or inherited.

## Growth Journey, Badge, and Learning Limits

- Impact Path is a synthetic local projection of confirmed lifetime Seeds, not measured impact,
  money, XP, a paid pass, or a production account history.
- The exact 16 proposed badges are private Ghaf product definitions. They are not government,
  tourism, UNESCO, school, venue, or environmental accreditations and do not prove character,
  mastery beyond their stated criteria, a visit, or real-world impact.
- Badge and path persistence across an app restart is a planned local prototype behavior, not yet
  implemented. There is no cloud sync, backup, account recovery, cross-device consistency, or
  production migration.
- Historical badge migration may use only provable immutable synthetic evidence. It cannot infer
  missing learning, action, Seed, visit, or maintenance history.
- Learning and activity completion are planned deterministic local events with zero Seeds and zero
  garden growth. The story-disabled/Parent-guided route is equal-credit, not a lesser option.
- The source ledger is research input. Links may change; factual, Arabic/UAE cultural,
  safeguarding, accessibility, and rights review are `NOT RUN`. Access dates are not Ghaf approval.
- Place-inspired stories require no GPS, location/venue check-in, Child photo, Child audio, map, or
  external web access. They cannot imply a physical visit, partnership, certification, official
  endorsement, or current venue availability.
- Badge art must be original. The prototype may not copy game-pass layouts, chests, crowns, rarity
  treatments, government seals, tourism branding, official logos, flags, or attraction imagery.
- The proposed Opening Moment and first-run introduction are presentation only. They cannot create
  a profile, mutate progress, request permissions, or delay a ready route for marketing.
- Manual introduction replay preserves all domain state. The Parent-authorized demo reset remains
  an explicit fixture reset and is distinct from ordinary restart/replay.
- `RevealBundle` is a planned single result surface, not a new source of truth. It must display the
  immutable receipt and preserve praise, Seed, garden, canopy, League, path/badge, and private
  Family Reward ordering.

No old pass may be copied into this table.

## Synthetic Access and Security Limits

The revised experiences must look and behave as separate protected roles, but P0 does not provide
production security.

- Parent email/phone identity, verification codes, PIN, passkey, biometric gate, sessions, and
  reauthentication are deterministic synthetic states.
- Child PINs and picture sequences do not establish identity, age, guardianship, or secure access.
- QR images and short pairing codes are fixtures; there is no camera scan, server exchange, code
  entropy guarantee, expiry service, encrypted device binding, or real Parent approval channel.
- Device lists, revocation, recovery, and permission changes affect only local prototype state.
- There is no multi-family tenancy, credential storage guarantee, account recovery service,
  intrusion protection, audit log, abuse prevention, or production authorization boundary.
- Children cannot reach Parent controls through normal prototype navigation, but this is a UX
  demonstration, not a security claim.

## Friendly League Limits

The Ghaf Family League is a deterministic local simulation, not a public or production social
network. Its research basis is mixed and indirect; the prototype cannot claim that competition will
improve routine, motivation, learning, or wellbeing for a particular Child.

- Each synthetic participating Child receives exactly five Parent-nominated Challenge Leaves.
  `Weekly Growth Score = confirmed Challenge Leaves ÷ 5 × 100`; maximum 100.
- Help and accessibility adaptations receive full credit. Extra tasks, raw Seeds, difficulty,
  speed, evidence, age, or money cannot improve rank. Ties share position.
- Weekly score resets; permanent Seeds and garden stages do not.
- Prayer, affection, emotional disclosure, relationship closeness, food consumption, private
  wellbeing, hygiene, disability-related routines, and recognition-only tasks are ineligible.
- Participants see only synthetic nickname, tree avatar, position, score, and completed Leaves.
  Task titles, evidence, age, accommodations, Seed totals, Parent notes, absence reasons, and money
  remain private.
- Prepared encouragement has no free text, replies, negative reaction, popularity count, or
  direct-message channel. Fixed reactions can still feel pressuring or sarcastic in context, so
  families must be able to mute them and named human review remains required.
- Rest, pause, and opt-out create no penalty, lost Seed, dead tree, public explanation, or reduced
  access. P0 simulates these controls; it does not implement real Child assent or guardian consent.
- The cooperative canopy accompanies ranking but does not prove that competition is harmless.

The League should be paused for human review when a Child or Parent reports distress, teasing,
family conflict, unsafe extra work, sleep loss, inaccessible opportunities, pressure to disclose,
or rank displacing task meaning. The prototype does not infer these conditions from activity,
voice, or facial data.

`challengeLeafEligible` is not `circleEligible`. The latter remains restricted to a coarse,
household-visible Green Impact activity event; neither field may expose the underlying task record.

## Family Reward and Money Limits

**Family Reward — المكافأة العائلية** is a private Parent promise, not a wallet, account, store,
payment product, wage, or entitlement guaranteed by Ghaf.

- P0 seeds amounts and milestones locally. It does not hold, transfer, settle, withdraw, refund,
  exchange, or custody money; connect to a bank; sell a gift card; verify that a reward was given;
  or enforce a promise outside the app.
- There is no universal Seed-to-AED exchange rate. League position never unlocks money, and a Child
  can unlock a personal plan regardless of rank.
- Amounts are visible only to the selected synthetic Child and guardians. Monetary information is
  absent from League and shared reactions.
- States are `Promised → Unlocked → Given`. The prototype must preserve an unlocked state and cannot
  remove it as punishment. Future plan changes are prospective; it does not silently rewrite an
  agreed milestone.
- Parent reauthentication and the monthly maximum promised amount are simulated decision guards,
  not affordability assessment, financial advice, consumer protection, or payment authorization.
- Food, water, clothing, shelter, sleep, healthcare, education, transport, safety, ordinary family
  contact, affection, and ordinary religious participation can never be conditioned on a reward.
- Prayer, affection, emotional disclosure, eating, body/health outcomes, relationship closeness,
  proof of love, and private wellbeing or disability-related activity cannot be monetized.
- Family Reward progress fails closed. Each contributing synthetic confirmation carries an
  eligibility decision tied to its immutable task version; unknown or prohibited contributions
  add zero. A landscape-stage plan uses eligible provenance, not the displayed stage alone.
- Families may choose a nonmonetary plan or no Family Reward. Praise and garden growth appear before
  a reward message to keep money secondary.

A Parent who cannot comfortably afford the maximum promise, a Child who feels pressure, a disputed
agreement, an unreachable target, indefinite payment expectation, or crowd-out of unpriced helpful
activity requires a human review and prospective pause. Earned Seeds, growth, and unlocked rewards
remain intact.

## Reward-Psychology Limits

Seeds, Challenge Leaves, League position, garden growth, and Family Rewards are not clinical
treatment, proof of virtue, measures of love/faith, predictions of behavior, or evidence that a
habit has formed.

- Different children respond differently; Parents can make tasks smaller, allow help, disable
  League, and pause future reward plans.
- A missed task creates no debt, demerit, punitive streak, public failure, red loser state, Seed
  deduction, dying tree, randomized reward, loot box, or artificial scarcity.
- An accepted task completed with permitted help earns its displayed award. Only a smaller task
  agreed before acceptance may display a smaller award.
- Recognition-only and maintenance rules remain separate from rewarded acquisition. No phase
  changes automatically, and past progress remains.
- External rewards can crowd out existing interest in some contexts. Ghaf therefore cannot promise
  internal motivation; meaningful choice, human praise, rest/opt-out, and periodic review remain
  necessary.

Ghaf is not a substitute for evidence-based parenting support, therapy, education planning, or
professional care.

## Child AI, Media, and Voice Limits

The P0 Ghaf Coach is prepared, deterministic, and limited to the current Parent-approved task. It
may simplify a task, show short steps, create an if–then cue, rehearse a curated phrase, respond to a
prepared fixture, offer one optional task-focused reflection, or direct the Child to an adult.

- Ages 6–8 receive slower prepared audio, one short instruction, curated intents only, and an early
  Ask an adult action.
- Ages 9–11 receive two or three steps, quick choices, and structured/template input.
- Ages 12–14 receive concise non-babyish wording and may see guardian-enabled bounded controls; no
  age receives unrestricted chat.
- The Coach never acts as a friend, companion, therapist, confidant, religious authority, teacher
  replacement, or substitute Parent; invites secrets/dependence; diagnoses; judges truth or faith;
  recognizes a face/voice; or infers emotion, personality, risk, or intent.

P0 push-to-talk is a visual and deterministic audio/transcript simulation. The visible recording
state, transcript, delete-before-send, replay, captions, slower playback, and send action do not
mean the microphone recorded a Child. There is no speech recognition, code-switch understanding,
voice analysis, upload, retention, ambient listening, or background recording. All photos, audio,
and transcripts are synthetic fixtures.

A future real provider or media path requires a separately approved production design, verified
guardian consent and age-appropriate Child assent, minimum data, reviewed retention/deletion,
strong media safeguards, no training/secondary use, monitoring/reporting, trusted-adult escalation,
and current UAE/provider review.

## Parent AI Limits

The P0 Ghaf Guide uses synthetic task and completion records. It may suggest a curated task, clarify
positive wording, offer a smaller step, draft descriptive praise, and summarize time-bounded
observable records. The Parent accepts any change.

It must not output normal/abnormal, lazy/defiant, a diagnosis, ADHD or another condition, a
developmental/risk/personality/emotion score, intent, truthfulness, religiosity, parenting quality,
League entitlement, or reward entitlement. It cannot replace a pediatrician, teacher, counselor,
qualified religious adviser, or financial adviser.

## Arabic, English, and Typography Limits

Revision 2 requires Alexandria for display type and Readex Pro for body/UI type in both languages,
true Arabic RTL, matched English LTR, generous Arabic line height, no artificial Arabic tracking,
tabular numerals, mixed-direction handling, scaling, and unclipped labels. These requirements are
implemented locally for the released R001 slice with scoped automated/web evidence. Direct native
visual rendering and every later screen remain unvalidated design targets.

- Font selection alone does not validate Arabic shaping, diacritics, mixed `AED 25`/`١٢٠ بذرة`,
  Android fallback, weight availability, screen-reader order, dynamic type, or long labels.
- Modern Standard Arabic remains the safe default for requirements and sensitive content.
- Light Emirati/Gulf greetings, code-switch fixtures, gendered wording, transliteration, and every
  prepared voice require named local review. P0 must not claim general dialect or code-switch
  understanding.

## Social, Data, and Privacy Limits

P0 has no real family invitations, accounts, contact graph, public discovery, profiles, direct
messages, comments, free text, location, school data, presence, reporting service, moderation, or
cross-device synchronization. League rows, reactions, permissions, household records, Parent notes,
and Family Reward plans are local synthetic fixtures.

The prototype has no basis to claim compliance with UAE child-digital-safety, personal-data,
financial, consumer, health, or education law. Before real child accounts, League sharing,
rewards, AI, photo/voice, notifications, or analytics, a separate production feature must define and
validate guardian consent, Child assent, age assurance, privacy by default, minimum collection,
purpose limitation, role authorization, media consent, retention/deletion, withdrawal,
export/correction, moderation/reporting, escalation, and provider handling.

If OpenAI services are considered for minors, re-check the current
[OpenAI Under 18 API Guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-checks/under-18-api-guidance)
and applicable terms at implementation time. A copied summary is not a compliance plan.

## Cultural, Religious, Food, and Wellbeing Limits

Modern Standard Arabic phrase options are prototype content, not one universal Emirati custom.
Dialect, greetings, wedding phrases, majlis practice, dress, photography, gahwa service, family
expectations, and voice differ by family, emirate, event, and context. Children do not handle hot
gahwa; faith content remains Parent-controlled, private, nonpunitive, ineligible for League/reward,
and outside AI judgment. Named UAE Arabic/cultural and qualified religious review remain required.

Ghaf may recognize safe food planning, table help, guardian-supervised leftover storage, or waste
reduction. It does not score body weight, calories, dieting, amount eaten, a clean plate, sleep,
mental health, emotion, fitness, nutrition, disability, or medical status. Food and healthcare are
never rewards or punishments; the Parent owns food-safety decisions.

## Sustainability and Impact Limits

The garden represents symbolic family growth. It does not mean Ghaf plants a tree, restores
habitat, offsets carbon, saves verified litres, or proves environmental change.

- A quantity appears only when a Parent confirms an observable value and is labeled self-reported
  or estimated activity.
- Environmental-impact language requires a separately approved method.
- Seeds, Challenge Leaves, League points, canopy leaves, and Family Rewards are not impact units.
- Children report leaks, electricity, chemicals, glass, sharps, batteries, unknown waste, and
  unsafe routes to an adult rather than touching or repairing them.

- Current source preserves the superseded ten-route Revision 1 baseline and implements only the

  remaining role shells or Growth Journey.
- Unreleased routes, navigation guards, Child access, League, reward plans, voice controls, Growth

- Current prototype domain state is local/in memory. Revision 3 proposes a small versioned local

  scope.
- Every new account, credential, pairing, task, reward, League row, reaction, badge, learning
  event, media item, and assistant response is synthetic or prepared as labelled.
- No native camera capture, QR scan, biometric verification, microphone recording, upload,
  background audio, payment API, or remote social service is promised.
- Web/source evidence cannot pass Android RTL, font, keyboard, Back, biometric, media, permission,
  reduced-motion, screen-reader, or physical-device requirements.
- Old Feature 002/003 tests and screenshots do not pass later Revision 2 or Revision 3 acceptance.
