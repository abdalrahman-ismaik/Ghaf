# Ghaf Plus Commercial Case

**Status:** competition pricing hypothesis and implemented Parent-only conversion preview

**Date:** 2026-09-08

Ghaf Plus answers a real product-capacity question without monetizing a Child's attention,
progress, safety, or dignity. The complete ad-free Ghaf journey remains free for one or two Child
profiles. A single proposed household subscription extends the intended production capacity to
three through six profiles, with no per-Child fee inside that limit.

The current build demonstrates this offer at Family Basics, where a Parent naturally decides how
many profiles the household needs. It does not sell, charge, activate, restore, or verify a
subscription and cannot create a third profile. Price, conversion, retention, cost, and profit
remain hypotheses until they are tested in an approved production program.

## Proposed Offer

| Plan              | Household capacity |  Proposed price | Product promise                                           |
| ----------------- | -----------------: | --------------: | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free              |   Up to 2 Children |           AED 0 | Complete current task-to-growth journey, ad-free          |
| Ghaf Plus monthly |       3–6 Children | AED 19.99/month | One household plan; no per-Child fee                      |
| Ghaf Plus annual  |       3–6 Children | AED 159.99/year | Same plan; AED 79.89 or 33% below twelve monthly payments |

The Plus advantage is capacity only. It does not change tasks, Seeds, Garden growth, League rank,
Family Rewards, AI safeguards, adult help, accessibility, privacy, offline fallback, or any Child
outcome. Commercial content appears only in verified Parent setup; Child surfaces contain no
price, trial, scarcity, upgrade, or purchase persuasion.

## Why This Strengthens the Competition Case

- **Clear conversion point:** the offer appears only when a Parent explores capacity beyond the
  generous two-profile free plan.
- **Household fairness:** one price covers the proposed larger household instead of charging for
  each additional Child.
- **Trust as differentiation:** both plans are ad-free, the free core is complete, and no Child is
  asked to influence a purchase.
- **Scalable product story:** the prototype shows where commercial conversion belongs while its
  current deterministic two-profile journey stays complete and reliable.
- **Auditable economics:** prices and examples use integer fils; the app and documentation never
  relabel gross arithmetic as profit.

These are design advantages, not evidence of market demand or a validated business model.

## Transparent Commercial Scenarios

These examples multiply the proposed price by an illustrative number of paying households. They
are gross billings arithmetic, not forecasts, recognized revenue, contribution margin, profit, or
valuation.

| Illustrative plan mix                     | Paying households | Gross arithmetic |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------: | ---------------: |
| Monthly plan for one month                |             1,000 |       AED 19,990 |
| Monthly plan retained for twelve payments |             1,000 |      AED 239,880 |
| Annual plan for one year                  |             1,000 |      AED 159,990 |
| Annual plan for one year                  |             5,000 |      AED 799,950 |
| Annual plan for one year                  |            10,000 |    AED 1,599,900 |

Actual profitability would require evidence for this equation:

```text
net contribution = collected billings
                 - store fees and taxes
                 - refunds and failed payments
                 - acquisition and retention costs
                 - infrastructure and AI costs
                 - support, safeguarding, legal, and operations costs
```

No value in that equation has been validated for Ghaf. Google Play service fees vary by program
and developer status, so this case assumes no specific rate. The UAE tax treatment also requires
professional review; the Federal Tax Authority's
[e-commerce VAT guidance](https://tax.gov.ae/DataFolder/Files/Pdf/E-Commerce%20-%20VAT%20Guide%20-%20EN%20-%2009%2008%202020.pdf)
describes a 5% standard rate for taxable supplies but is not product-specific tax advice.

## Required Production Path

An Android release selling this digital capacity would need to follow the current Google Play
[Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738) and use a
proper subscription lifecycle. Google's
[billing architecture](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/) recommends a secure
backend for purchase verification and lifecycle handling, while the
[subscription policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533)
requires clear price, billing frequency, renewal, management, and cancellation information.

Before activation, Ghaf needs all of the following in a separate approved feature:

1. stable dynamic Child and household identifiers instead of the two synthetic profile IDs;
2. complete per-profile access, task, Seed, Garden, League, Reward, privacy, reset, and migration
   behavior for profiles three through six;
3. a configured Play product and Google Play Billing integration with test accounts;
4. server-side purchase verification, account binding, acknowledgement, real-time lifecycle
   handling, restore, cancellation, grace, hold, refund, and revocation behavior;
5. lapse-safe data rules that never delete a profile or remove earned Seeds and growth;
6. Parent reauthentication for plan management and no Child-facing purchase controls;
7. updated privacy, terms, store disclosure, support, incident, tax, security, safeguarding, and
   data-retention reviews; and
8. physical Android Back, TalkBack, font-scale, offline, interruption, process-death, purchase,
   renewal, cancellation, and restoration evidence.

Google Play's [billing testing guidance](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/test)
must be part of that validation. Until every gate is approved, the competition build must keep the
local preview and may not claim that Ghaf Plus is purchasable.

## Judge-Ready Explanation

> Ghaf keeps its complete, ad-free family growth journey free for one or two Children. At the
> natural family-capacity decision, Parents can preview Ghaf Plus: one proposed household plan for
> up to six Children, with no per-Child fee and no Child-facing sales pressure. The AED pricing and
> scenarios are transparent hypotheses, while this competition build processes no purchase. That
> gives Ghaf a credible path to sustainable revenue without turning Child attention, safety, or
> progress into the product.
