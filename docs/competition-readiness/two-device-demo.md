# Android demonstration and two-device validation

The target is **one complete 2–3 minute journey on the primary Android phone**. The secondary
Android independently verifies installation, touch, responsive layout, persistence and reliability.
The two installations have separate local state. Live synchronization is deferred.

This document is a proposed operator and acceptance script, not completed rehearsal evidence.
The current build supports the canonical task/help/Parent-confirmation/growth flow locally, but
progress restoration, a durable private memory leaf/timeline and generalized recommendation
presentation remain gaps. See [current QA](qa-report.md). Do not narrate proposed features as built.

The supplied [orientation](../SMAC%202026/SMAC2026_Orientation_Day.pdf), page 12, specifies a
2–3 minute submission video and September 16 for qualified teams' on-campus demonstration.
The official live speaking allowance is unspecified; the user has chosen the same 2–3 minute
main-flow target. September 8 submission has passed; this package does not imply a late or revised
submission is accepted.

## The family story

“A family agrees on one small action. The Child can ask for help. The Parent recognizes the
contribution, their Ghaf garden grows, and a private memory preserves the shared experience.”

The final clause is a target requiring a new accepted memory story. In the current build, say
“their garden records the confirmed action” and show the actual growth; do not call an internal
event log a completed family memory timeline. Use the canonical safe recycling task to show
cooperation. Ordinary affection, disclosure and connection-only activities remain recognition-only
and cannot create Seeds or persistent landscape/canopy growth.

AI should make a next step easier to understand. The Parent decides whether a task is suitable
and whether it is complete. Prepared suggestions must be labeled prepared; a rationale must name
actual selection inputs, never invented psychology or relationship scores. The virtual garden is
symbolic and does not establish real trees planted or measured environmental impact.

## Preparation

Install the exact same candidate APK on both phones using the
[Android build and acceptance guide](android-build-and-rehearsal.md). Record actual models, Android
versions, app commit, APK hash, locale and flags; none are assumed. Use adult presenters and the
synthetic family only. Keep the prepared assets local and all independent release flags at their
approved settings. Do not enable a blocked experience for its appearance alone.

Before rehearsal, prove the app launches without Metro and without internet. Reset to the signed-out
Arabic-first state. Use existing separate Parent and Child access for the local handoffs; there is
no in-app role toggle. Measure the actual time those transitions take. If the complete flow cannot
fit, shorten narration and prepare approved fixtures before presentation; do not bypass role guards
or quietly edit the recording into a claim of uninterrupted live execution.

Have three clean plastic demonstration items, a charger and an offline operator script available.
Do not handle unknown waste, glass or hazardous objects. Device owners may silence unrelated
personal notifications through their normal settings. The secondary phone is a tested spare and
QA device, not a staged live recipient of the primary phone's actions.

## 150-second main storyboard

This is the target for both a permitted video and the live core. Each proposed row has a gate.

| Time      | Visible action                                                              | Family value and capability gate                                                                   |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Enter the prepared synthetic family; show the living Ghaf identity          | Arabic-first family connection and UAE identity; existing access must work                         |

| 0:40–1:00 | Child chooses the approved action and requests permitted help               | Choice and support are part of success; retain the accepted award                                  |

| 1:15–1:40 | Parent names what happened and confirms once                                | Human recognition precedes the single authorized growth result                                     |
| 1:40–2:00 | Show the visible garden change and private memory leaf                      | Current growth works; durable memory must be implemented and tested before claiming it             |
| 2:00–2:15 | Open the saved family memory/timeline                                       | Proposed private route; a replay grants no additional progression                                  |
| 2:15–2:30 | Close on family bonds and UAE Year of Family 2026                           | One clear family story; no organizer endorsement or proven wellbeing claim                         |

The UAE's official [Year of Family page](https://u.ae/en/Year-of-family/Family-year-2026) links the
2026 theme to family bonds, shared identity and family stability. Ghaf can explain its alignment
without claiming government affiliation.

If memory/rationale work misses the freeze, remove those claims and show the actual prepared
Guide, supported completion and garden. Record the missing target in the readiness verdict.
That reduced sequence is a truthful fallback, not acceptance of the full requested experience.
Do not fill its time with unbuilt map, wallet, calendar or chat screens.

## Physical acceptance record

Target ten complete primary-phone rehearsals, each 2–3 minutes, after the final build. Record ten
actual observations, including cold launch, background/resume, airplane mode, interrupted completion,
restart after recognition and reset. Do not call a seeded start a cold-install test. Record any
operator help. Test the secondary independently on the same APK, including its actual narrower or
wider layout, text scaling, Back, touch, persistence and reset.

| Secondary installation and reliability | To record   | Same candidate    | To record      | —       | —                     | NOT RUN           |

Screenshots and source checks cannot pass Android RTL, TalkBack, keyboard, Back, permission,
reduced-motion or physical acceptance. Named Arabic/cultural review and genuine student code
