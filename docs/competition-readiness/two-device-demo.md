## The family story

“A family agrees on one small action. The Child can ask for help. The Parent recognizes the
contribution, and their symbolic Ghaf garden grows.”

Use the canonical safe recycling task to show cooperation. The current demonstration contains no
saved memory leaf or timeline. An internal recognition record is not that user-facing feature.
Ordinary affection, disclosure and connection-only activities remain recognition-only and cannot
create Seeds or persistent landscape/canopy growth.

AI should make a next step easier to understand. The Parent decides whether a task is suitable
and whether it is complete. Prepared suggestions must be labeled prepared; a rationale must name
actual selection inputs, never invented psychology or relationship scores. The virtual garden is
symbolic and does not establish real trees planted or measured environmental impact.
