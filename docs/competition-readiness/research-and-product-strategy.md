# Ghaf competition product strategy

Ghaf's competition story is a visible agreement between a Parent and a Child: choose an achievable
action, obtain useful help, complete it together in the real world, receive Parent recognition,
and see symbolic garden growth. One primary Android phone demonstrates the entire local,
role-separated journey in the user's requested **2–3 minutes**. A secondary phone independently
checks installation, responsiveness, touch, restart and reset; it does not share the primary
phone's household state. The exact phone models are not yet known.

The current selected batch prioritizes a fast, understandable local journey and a real Android
artifact. The user explicitly deferred recovery014 until native validation and selected three
synthetic no-auth profiles, shorter onboarding and Arabic narration repair. Runtime2ecea74 now
implements the isolated demo doorway and silent three-moment story; full checks pass147files/
1914tests. The user rejected all three Arabic narration candidates; English remains unreviewed, and short
alternative Arabic narrator auditions are authorized. Physical APK/
phone acceptance is still pending; test counts do not substitute for it.

Research on the suggested ideas is preserved below. Research and a roadmap do not mean those
features were implemented. The earlier stabilization work repaired existing logic; the current
Feature015 change improves demo access and onboarding. A private durable memory leaf remains a
proposal, and native validation will not automatically activate recovery or memory work. Calendar
is optional only after core acceptance and explicit selection. Study, money literacy, maps, Google
integrations and chat remain later/post-competition. No backend, live tracking, payments, social
networking or real family data enters this batch. A prize or developmental benefit is not guaranteed.

The strongest next product hypothesis is the bounded **“Do the first step together”** interaction:
the Child requests one concrete kind of help on the approved task, the Parent acknowledges that
same private request, and the Child completes with the agreed support. It makes Parent participation
visible without chat, sync, new points or reward pressure. This remains **unselected**, with the
specific before/after and failure cases in [A's product review](workstreams/a-product-service-review.md)
and [C's proposal](workstreams/c-product-refinement.md). It needs an explicit user/student scope
choice and committed story before implementation; do not present it in the current demo as built.

## Evidence and its limits

The evidence supports design principles and learning techniques. It does not validate Ghaf as an
intervention, establish a UAE family effect size, or show that a calendar, chatbot or points system
improves cognition. Observational association, experimental findings, professional guidance and
product design judgments are kept distinct.

| Evidence                                                             | Finding and strength                                                                                                                                 | Ghaf implication                                                                                     | Limitation                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Bradshaw et al., 2025, 238-sample parenting meta-analysis            | Autonomy support was positively associated with well-being; psychological control with ill-being, across multiple cultural/developmental groups.[^2] | Offer meaningful Parent-approved choices, rationale, help and renegotiation                          | Mostly relational evidence; an app benefit or causality does not follow        |
| Breitwieser and Reinelt, 2026, child implementation-intention review | If–then planning can support goal attainment; effects vary.[^3]                                                                                      | Let the Child choose a concrete cue and one attainable next step                                     | No universal habit deadline or guaranteed success                              |
| Deci, Koestner and Ryan, 1999                                        | Expected tangible, contingent rewards can reduce subsequent intrinsic interest in experimental settings.[^4]                                         | Keep Seeds predictable and secondary; do not purchase affection or make every activity transactional | Older, heterogeneous experiments; not a finding that every reward is harmful   |
| Cerasoli, Nicklin and Ford, 2014                                     | Motivation and incentives can jointly relate to performance; their roles differ for quality and quantity.[^5]                                        | Assess meaningful completion, comprehension and choice rather than accumulating taps                 | Includes work, school and physical domains; not a child-app trial              |
| Fryer, 2010/2011, randomized school incentives                       | In those school trials, rewarding controllable inputs was more effective than rewarding output scores.[^6]                                           | Prefer practice plans, strategies and agreed effort opportunities over a cash-for-marks default      | Specific US school settings; not a universal ban on celebrating achievement    |
| Agarwal, Nunes and Blunt, 2021, classroom retrieval review           | Fifty experiments with 5,374 learners generally supported retrieval practice; only 6% of experiments were in non-WEIRD countries.[^7]                | Short recall attempts with feedback; revisit material later                                          | Limited cultural coverage; not evidence for generated answers or a timer alone |
| Mawson and Kang, 2025, classroom spacing meta-analysis               | Twenty-two reports found an average benefit of distributed practice, with substantial context variation.[^8]                                         | Plan revisits across days instead of rewarding long uninterrupted sessions                           | Does not prescribe one optimal schedule for every Child                        |
| Kaiser and Menkhoff, 2020, school financial education                | Thirty-seven experiments/quasi-experiments showed larger effects on knowledge than behavior; RCT estimates were smaller.[^9]                         | Teach one decision, practice it, and discuss it with a Parent                                        | A balance dashboard alone is not a financial curriculum                        |
| CFPB developmental framework                                         | Financial capability includes executive function, habits/norms and knowledge/decision skills.[^10]                                                   | Adapt planning and examples to age; provide hands-on family practice                                 | A US educational framework, not a Ghaf or UAE outcome study                    |
| UNICEF communication guidance; AAP family media guidance             | Active listening, understandable language, family-agreed routines and reviewing media rules are recommended.[^11][^12]                               | Design brief invitations to talk and act together, with clear stopping points                        | Professional guidance; adding chat does not establish stronger relationships   |
| UNICEF AI guidance 3.0                                               | Child-centered AI calls for safety, privacy, inclusion, transparency and accountability.[^13]                                                        | Keep bounded task support, Parent authority and prepared fallback                                    | Guidance is not a certification or proof of legal compliance                   |

## A distinctive product proposition

Family calendars, chores/allowance tools and location sharing already have established products.
Cozi describes shared calendars and lists; Greenlight describes chores and allowance tools; Life360
describes family location sharing.[^14][^15][^16] These vendor pages establish overlapping features,
not comparative effectiveness, regional availability or superiority.

Ghaf can distinguish itself through the **relationship between the steps**: a Child can ask for
help without losing the promised award; a Parent responds with a specific acknowledgment; private
progress becomes a cooperative UAE landscape; sensitive family experiences stay private. Arabic
interaction quality and the visible distinction between AI suggestions and human decisions make
that proposition tangible.

The memorable judge moment should be a Child tapping “I need help,” the Parent responding, and the
same accepted task receiving full credit after supported completion. The garden change then explains
what the app remembers. It should be possible to describe the entire sequence in one sentence.

## Feature decisions

### 1. One complete local family journey — core priority

Use the same primary Android installation for Parent selection and approval, Child acceptance and
help, and Parent confirmation through the existing access handoffs. No in-app role toggle or
second state authority is needed. The secondary phone is an independent installation and
responsiveness/recovery check. Neither phone represents live synchronization, a shared backend,
or delivery to another device.

First establish a reproducible install and baseline, then close the local progress-recovery gap.
The smallest Spec Kit story must define exactly which synthetic task/confirmation records survive
restart, their versioned validation and migration, safe storage-failure behavior, profile and family
binding, duplicate handling, and reset invalidation. Derive progress from accepted evidence rather
than persisting arbitrary screen balances. Do not persist privileged sessions, credentials, media,
or Child-private assistant content as a shortcut. Test cold start, process death, corrupt and
unknown versions, interrupted writes, family replacement, and stale asynchronous work after reset.
Existing directory and remembered-access behavior stays governed by its current contract.

Next make the available recommendation and its reason understandable. Existing Parent Guide
responses are prepared; the profile helper ranks curated categories from allowlisted selections.
Neither is a generalized live Connection Coach. A short rationale should explain only the actual
curated basis, admit fallibility, and leave Parent selection and review intact. Recommendation
generalization or a new rationale behavior requires its own smallest Spec Kit story before code;
no model call, backend, inference about personality, or automatic assignment is needed.

The climax stays **Parent confirmation → specific praise → +12 Seeds → Garden growth** for
`task_recycling_p0_v1`, including permitted-help completion. The current default display is 48→60
and the Mangrove track is 48/60→60/60. The gated lifetime 108→120 fixture is a separate authority
and must not replace the numbers on the demonstrated default screen.

A **private durable memory leaf** is proposed as a small acknowledgment of that confirmed shared
action. No such user-facing feature exists today. Its own story must define the minimum reviewed
synthetic content, Parent/Child visibility, profile binding, durability, deletion/reset and
idempotency before implementation. Derive it once from the accepted confirmation; opening,
replaying or restoring a leaf must never award Seeds or mutate Garden, canopy, League, badges or
Family Reward progress. It is not a new ledger of Child disclosures, an affection score, or a
public timeline. If its bounded story and native checks cannot finish before freeze, omit the leaf
from the live demo and label it proposed in the product discussion.

Feature 008 connection ideas remain private Parent recognition-only planning previews. They earn
zero Seeds, Garden, canopy, League or reward progress and cannot be used to manufacture this growth
climax. Preserve all independent R002b default-off flags. Each selected change is one named
student's reviewable task with actual AI prompts and contributions recorded; this roadmap does not
authorize agents to generate the complete feature set.

### 2. Local family agenda — optional only after the core passes

Consider one small local agenda only after the complete core journey, recovery and physical Android
gates pass and capacity remains before the September 14 freeze. It needs a separate bounded Spec Kit
story. Use one schedule authority and contextual views: Parent Home shows the next agreed
activity, Parent Tasks offers agenda/week views, Child Today shows only that Child's approved plan,
and task details expose the same date. A month grid is an optional navigation tool, not the default
landing page in every tab. Family connection rhythms must not silently become recurring assignments.

An entry represents an agreed window, not proof of completion. Support no-date tasks, all-day
activities, rescheduling, cancellation and a visible timezone. Use `Asia/Dubai` as the demo fixture
timezone; distinguish stored instants from date-only values and test display in another timezone.
No overdue shaming, deduction, automatic approval or extra League credit follows from a date.

Keep the competition option entirely local. A Parent-controlled one-time calendar export is a
separate post-competition proposal. Preview exactly what leaves Ghaf and choose a deliberately generic title; exclude Child
names, task evidence, sensitive categories, accommodations, reward amounts and assistant content.
An exported copy can remain in another calendar after deletion in Ghaf, so describe it as a copy,
not sync. Test duplicate import behavior in the chosen client rather than assuming `.ics` identifiers
guarantee deduplication.

A true Google Calendar integration stays post-competition and requires a separately approved OAuth flow, token lifecycle,
idempotent event mapping and explicit ownership of changes. Google's documentation recommends the
narrowest required scopes; an app-created secondary calendar is a candidate for evaluation instead
of requesting access to every calendar. Incremental sync has token-expiry recovery requirements.[^17][^18]
Google Calendar never becomes the authority for Seeds or completion. Do not connect real accounts
or publish events during this inspection.

### 3. Study support — later planning and comprehension proposal

The useful study loop is **choose → plan → try to recall → check → revisit**. A Child selects an
approved topic, agrees a manageable session, attempts a few reviewed questions, sees explanatory
feedback, and schedules a later revisit. The Parent sees an agreed plan and a request for support,
not an inferred attention, intelligence, emotion or motivation score.

Use locally authored, curriculum-reviewed sample material. A focus timer is optional, pausable and
never completion evidence. Leaving the app, using an accessibility aid or asking for help cannot
delete progress. Avoid camera monitoring, screen policing and background usage surveillance.
A later small learning sequence can demonstrate the pattern; it is outside this competition slice.
Do not build a school LMS, subject marketplace or unrestricted AI tutor.

Custom goals should be jointly understood and changeable prospectively. Prefer “practice this
strategy on three agreed occasions” or “explain one example and identify a question” over “score
95% or lose the reward.” An exam result may be celebrated without turning marks into a public rank,
identity label or money-conversion rule. Basic education, safety and parental affection never depend
on a goal. Under the current Family Reward exclusions, academic goals must not silently contribute
eligible reward Seeds: any proposed nonessential celebration policy needs an explicit reviewed
eligibility decision, and protected/unknown contributions remain zero.

The existing Mangrove learning package is a possible later demonstration of these ideas:
it already links sustainability to a finite accessible learning route. It remains gated until its
content and native review requirements pass. Adding generic school features before completing that
existing path would duplicate unfinished work.

### 4. Money literacy — later practical decisions, separate from Seeds

Propose a **money practice plan** with a Parent-set synthetic allowance, a Child's optional saving
goal, a simple allocation exercise and one short conversation. The interface can show “available
to plan,” “set aside” and “planned spending” without pretending Ghaf holds money. A Parent may record
an external allowance transfer as self-reported; no bank balance, card or custody claim follows.

For a 6–8 presentation, use concrete choices and a small number of amounts. For 9–11, compare two
options and revisit a saving goal. For 12–14, introduce a bounded budget and tradeoff explanation.
These are interface proposals, not assessments of an individual Child's ability. A zero allowance
or a family that does not use pocket money must have an equally complete learning route.

Keep money records, Family Reward promises, lifetime Seeds and League scores separate. There is
no universal Seed-to-AED exchange rate, peer balance ranking, investment return promise, borrowing,
debt punishment, crypto wallet or incentive to spend. The virtual-currency template can inform
numeric hierarchy, but its transfer/trading/product vocabulary should not enter Ghaf.

No evidence reviewed establishes that tying all allowance to chores is necessary. Prefer a Parent's
explicit household policy and predictable discussion, with basic needs protected. A later
extension should demonstrate one allocation decision and what the Child learned. Money literacy
and payments do not enter the current competition slice.

### 5. Maps — post-competition family discovery proposal

There are two different products hidden in the map idea. **Family discovery** helps a Parent choose
a suitable place or prepare a supervised activity. **Location tracking** continuously or repeatedly
collects a Child's whereabouts. The first can strengthen the sustainability story; the second adds
privacy, safeguarding, permission, retention and native-background complexity without strengthening
the canonical task demonstration.

For a later separately specified slice, start with a curated place card, a map/list alternative,
an accessible at-home learning option and
a Parent-owned “Open in Google Maps” action. Google Maps URLs work across platforms and do not
require a Google API key.[^19] Send a reviewed public place or destination only. Do not pass a Child
identifier or coordinates. Google Maps may itself use the adult device's location according to its
own settings; Ghaf must not claim this handoff is wholly private or offline.

Distance, location permission and a map arrival never confirm a task, earn Seeds, prove a visit or
unlock a badge. Adults own transport, outdoor safety and suitability. An offline place description
and list route remain usable when Maps cannot open. A map is not an emergency or child-safety service.

If location sharing is pursued later, require its own specification and review: purpose limitation,
visible status, consent and age-appropriate assent, session expiry, revocation, minimal precision
and retention, access logs and failure behavior. The UK ICO's code recommends off-by-default
geolocation and clear tracking indications; it is a useful design comparator, not UAE law.[^20]
The UAE has its own Child Digital Safety law, whose applicability and current implementation must
be assessed for a real service.[^21] Do not enable location collection in this synthetic prototype.

### 6. Communication — preserve local help; defer chat

The best first communication feature is a task-bound exchange: “Please help me with this step,”
“Can we choose another time?” and a Parent acknowledgment. It has a clear recipient, purpose and
ending. The existing local help/request flow already carries much of that value; make it clear
and recoverable within the role-separated journey. No messaging inbox, live delivery or social
networking enters the competition slice.

A later private Parent–Child conversation feature needs explicit recipient binding, visibility,
retention, deletion, blocking/reporting where applicable, non-response handling and a trusted-adult
route. Do not promise instant delivery, emergency monitoring or confidentiality the system cannot
provide. Quiet hours, no read-receipt pressure, no engagement streaks and no rewards for disclosure
help align the design with its purpose. An AI summary of private conversation is a separate decision,
not a default convenience.

Child-to-child free messaging is the lowest priority. It changes the safeguarding and moderation
problem substantially. Keep the existing invite-only synthetic League and curated encouragement
identifiers; do not infer permission for free messages from membership in a family group.

## Validation of the product idea

Use adult team members with synthetic profiles for the immediate rehearsal. Ask them to identify
the next action, explain who confirms it, request help and describe what the garden means. Record
where an operator prompt was needed, where wording was misunderstood and where state was lost.
These observations can improve usability without claiming developmental efficacy.

A later family study should be separately consented and designed with appropriate child expertise.
Useful hypotheses include whether the Child understands the plan, whether help-seeking is easier,
whether the Parent gives more specific feedback, and whether either person experiences pressure.
The objective is improved offline cooperation, not maximum time in the app. A small pilot can
discover friction; it cannot substantiate broad improvements in cognition, mental health, grades,
family bonds or environmental impact.

For study features, measure comprehension and delayed recall using reviewed material. For money
learning, measure the ability to explain a tradeoff rather than the amount saved. For sustainability,
record a Parent-confirmed observable activity separately from a symbolic tree. No conversion to
carbon, water or real trees is justified without an approved measurement method.
