## Purpose and composition

Present the private invite-only five-Leaf family League without turning it into a public contest.
The naturally scrolling surface contains one active-Child weekly summary, five visible Leaf markers,
the strict privacy-projected participant rows, normalized score and shared-position information,
and a short privacy explanation. The Arabic physical tab order remains
`الدوري | حديقتي | اليوم` from left to right.

The visual system extends the approved Ghaf Soft Geometric language: warm pearl ground, forest and
mangrove surfaces, saffron only for weekly progress, Alexandria headings, Readex Pro controls and
body copy, code-native icons, restrained depth, and no podium or public-leaderboard treatment.

## Data and behavior

The route consumes a deterministic presentation adapter over the existing Family League privacy
projector. A provenance-tagged synthetic reset summary records only participant identity, confirmed
Leaf count, score, and position: Salem starts at four of five Challenge Leaves and 80 points. It
does not manufacture hidden task history. The canonical `task_recycling_p0_v1` recognition receipt
may derive only Salem's fifth Leaf and 100 points after its full task, transaction, growth, canopy,
and household Green projection provenance passes. Other invitees remain bounded synthetic summary
evidence. The screen never confirms a Leaf, calculates a reward transaction, creates a task, or
writes League state.

Rows may expose only approved nickname, tree-avatar token, weekly position, normalized score, and
confirmed Challenge Leaf count. Never expose task titles or history, Seeds, badges, media,
reflections, Parent notes, ages, completion times, Family Reward data, or protected categories.
Green Circle remains `/circle`; Shared Growth remains an additive nested surface and is never
renamed to League.

## Navigation and states

Allow only Child capability, the active profile, and the default-off visibility gate. Today and
Garden pass the active profile through current store authority; invalid direct entry returns to
`/child`. The League tab is enabled only while the gate is on. Tab changes use no custom sliding
animation. The surface keeps the fixed Child bottom navigation and natural central scrolling.

Required states: reset fixture (4/5 for Salem), recognized fixture (5/5 for Salem), another active
Child, shared ties, loading, deterministic offline-ready, projection error with a safe return to
Today, no eligible week with the same safe return, interrupted recovery, and reduced-motion
equivalence. Missing decorative art never blocks the data or actions.

## Accessibility, copy, and review

Use one screen heading, meaningful list semantics, explicit current-Child and shared-position text,
five non-color Leaf states, 48×48 touch targets, 4.5:1 normal-text contrast, and readable status
copy. Reflow at 320, 360, 390, 430, and 768 widths and 200% text without horizontal overflow or
fixed-height copy. Arabic and English use centralized resources; Arabic copy and family terminology
remain subject to named human review.

## Acceptance

Prove the exact 4/5→5/5 and 80→100 projection from one valid recognition receipt; no change for an
unrelated, malformed, duplicate, or cross-profile receipt; five-Leaf cap; shared ties; no speed
tiebreak; full help/adaptation credit; strict allowlisted row fields; default-off fallback; role and
profile guards; reset determinism; Arabic physical tab order; English LTR reflow; reduced motion;
and zero mutations from viewing. Canonical 390×844 and physical Android/TalkBack evidence remain
pending until captured.
