## Cross-phase controls

1. **Authority control:** remote behavior is the current behavioral baseline; approved canonical documents are the product baseline; local commits and R002 exports remain candidate evidence until explicitly promoted.
2. **Semantic stop:** any unapproved conflict involving League/Shared Growth, `task_recycling_p0_v1`, progression, RevealBundle, Parent approval/help, roles/profile isolation, voice, reset/fixtures, privacy/consent, badges, or missing design authority stops the affected phase.
3. **Commit control:** reconstruct approved additive slices on the remote baseline. Do not cherry-pick the six broad local commits. Keep domain, migration, configuration, route, visual, and documentation changes independently reviewable.
4. **Validation control:** every phase starts from a recorded passing baseline and reports each command/evidence gate as `PASSED`, `FAILED`, `BLOCKED`, or `NOT RUN`. Pre-existing failures are distinguished from regressions.
5. **Rollback control:** the rollback unit is the last independently passing commit, not a manual whole-file “ours/theirs” selection. Persistent-state phases require explicit backward/read recovery evidence.
6. **Scope control:** one Expo/React Native app, deterministic local synthetic data, no production accounts/backend/payment/public leaderboard/live Child media processing, and no unreleased screen implementation.
