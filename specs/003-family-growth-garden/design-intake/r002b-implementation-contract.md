## Approved projection contract

### Schema-3 audit record

At verified baseline `0501cf3`, Salem's personal `earnedSeeds` scalar and the current Mangrove
track's `cumulativeSeeds` scalar are both `48`, but the recognition service reads and updates them
independently. Their equality is deterministic fixture alignment, not a ledger invariant. The reset
fixture has an empty recognition ledger, no completed-stage archive, and no profile/reset epoch, so
stored Seed transactions sum to `0` at reset and `12` after the canonical approval rather than
reconstructing either displayed total.

The application has no durable profile store: Zustand and every mock provider reset from local
synthetic fixtures. No archived historical `60` is verified. The authorization therefore permits
one Salem-only `60` carry-forward solely as an **approved synthetic fixture assumption**, never as
claimed task, mastery, learning, or real-user history. Alya retains only her own opening `36`;
League-only cousin profiles and unknown profiles are ineligible.

One authoritative `+12` Parent approval has separate, simultaneous projections:

| Projection                |      Before |                   After |
| ------------------------- | ----------: | ----------------------: |
| Current Mangrove stage    |       48/60 |         60/60, archived |
| Confirmed lifetime Seeds  |         108 |                     120 |
| Water & Coast Impact Path | not entered | current chapter 120→180 |

`lifetimeSeeds` is derived only from unique committed Seed ledger entries in the active profile
epoch. It is not writable, spendable, reset within an epoch, or copied from a screenshot. A legacy
carry-forward is permitted only as one immutable, versioned, idempotent synthetic migration receipt
with explicit profile and fixture provenance. It may not invent task, mastery, learning, League,
canopy, Challenge, or Family Reward history.
