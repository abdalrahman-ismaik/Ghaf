# Family practice source and adaptation record

Reviewed primary/official source pages on 2026-09-13. This record supports FR018–FR020.
These are optional family adaptations, not tested Ghaf interventions or curriculum certification.
No effect size, promised grade, wellbeing improvement or family-relationship outcome is attributed
to this implementation. No source author endorsed Ghaf.

| Activity                | Source-backed strategy                                                                                                                                                       | Scope and adaptation                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Make one small plan     | Plan, monitor and review learning. [EEF toolkit](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/metacognition-and-self-regulation) | Evidence concerns teaching in curriculum contexts. The three-step family version and supported communication choices are Ghaf design adaptations.                             |
| Recall, then check      | Retrieve previously taught material. [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1)                                                                       | A 2007 educational guide. Use parent/teacher-checked material, offer hints and correction; no attempt score or mastery inference.                                             |
| Explain one example     | Use worked examples and explanatory questions. [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1)                                                             | A brief activity cannot establish learning efficacy; a Parent models an explanation or refers an unresolved question to a teacher.                                            |
| Make room to revisit    | Distribute study across time. [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1)                                                                              | Families choose another session. No prescribed optimal interval or claim that tapping Finish constitutes a later review; reminders are on paper, not scheduled by this guide. |
| Take listening turns    | Listen, clarify and respect perspectives. [UNICEF parenting guidance](https://www.unicef.org/parenting/child-care/11-tips-communicating-your-teen)                           | Guidance focuses on teenagers, not an all-age randomized trial. The family chooses neutral topics; sharing, spoken responses and eye contact are optional.                    |
| Enjoy a shared interest | Share interests and everyday enjoyment. [UNICEF parenting guidance](https://www.unicef.org/parenting/child-care/11-tips-communicating-your-teen)                             | An adult adapts to age and access needs. No purchase, affection, disclosure or performance is required.                                                                       |

Each session has Choose / Try / Review stages and two equally valid versions. The supported
version permits pointing, drawing, reading aloud, help with writing or movement, seated activity
and passing. The versions share the same optional acknowledgement; neither creates persisted
progress, Seeds, badges, League activity or any prize. No answers, recordings or emotional states
are collected. Back revisits a step; Stop closes with a neutral message; changing activity resets
temporary step state. Sources open only after the user taps the link; the guide works offline.

The Arabic resources use Modern Standard Arabic. Named Arabic, UAE-family, educator,
safeguarding and accessibility review: **NOT RUN**. Physical Android, TalkBack and native Back
acceptance: **NOT RUN** pending the root's recorded evidence. Automated and browser results
belong in the Feature017 validation record, not inferred from this source inspection.
