# Decisions and research boundaries

The owner selected points 8–12 and explicitly instructed proceeding from the saved
proposals on 2026-09-13. The earlier exclusions on academic prizes and peer chat
are superseded only by this feature's narrow contract and Constitution 2.2.0.

- Reuse the existing local synchronous repository seam. Demo entry and adult pilot
  use isolated memory; ordinary local family mode may persist study records on the
  device. No student record is uploaded or copied into adult accounts.
- Academic agreements have an explicit revision, both parties' approval, immutable
  accepted terms and idempotent Parent acknowledgement. A below-target result may
  be retried without loss. Nonessential prizes remain private and outside the app.
- Keep separate study authority: existing Family Rewards exclude academic tasks,
  and existing Seeds, recognition, growth, League and badge rules are unchanged.
- Extend Feature 016's Auth/RPC service with additive SQL. The messaging provider is
  not configured in this workspace; the adult pilot's credentials grant no messaging
  authority. Local service tests cannot pass hosted delivery or two-device gates.
- The finite study loop uses planning, retrieval, checking, explanation and revisiting.
  [WWC's study guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) supports spacing,
  retrieval and explanatory questions in instructional settings. These findings do
  not establish effectiveness of this app or certify its prepared example content.
- [EEF's metacognition guidance](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/metacognition-and-self-regulation)
  supports explicit planning, monitoring and evaluation within subject learning.
  Ghaf does not inherit the toolkit's aggregate effect estimates.

Detailed activity sources, scope and limitations are in `practice-research.md`.
