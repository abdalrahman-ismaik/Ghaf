# Masroofi surface brief

Mode: Operate. Extend Ghaf's botanical world; this is a private, contextual money-practice
surface, not a replacement visual identity or a separate app.

The card is the visual anchor: dark forest substrate, gold geometric chip, fine dune contours,
an abstract Ghaf seal and repeating woven diamonds along the lower edge. These are original
SVG geometries inspired by UAE landscapes/craft, not an authenticated traditional artifact or
official national/financial emblem. Native text carries the title, holder and demo marking.

Palette and typography are inherited from src/design/tokens.ts. Alexandria carries titles;
Readex Pro carries controls, names and tabular amounts. The card approximates physical-card
proportions at normal phone widths, expands for text, and keeps its holder name readable.
Decorative vectors are inaccessible; the visible title/holder/demo text remains semantic.

Parent sequence: choose profile → card → activate or balance → fixed task reward → purchase
rules → practice funds → activity. Child sequence: card → available balance → earned credit →
finite practice shop → readable rules → activity. Native switches and explicit Save apply
prospective controls. Amount choices and Child selectors wrap; no horizontally scrolling controls.

Money is not drawn into the artwork. The visible balance is a native live projection below the
card, and every state explains its simulated status. New typography/palette tokens, issuer
logos, card credentials, randomized reveal motion and cultural-authenticity claims are excluded.
Physical Android and named Arabic/UAE review remain distinct from browser inspection.
