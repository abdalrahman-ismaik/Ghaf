## Verdict

Ghaf has a distinctive, place-specific emotional shell—the entry landscape, family Ghaf canopy, Mangrove growth, and cooperative circle are memorable and coherent. Its operational middle is much less authored: task drafting, review, and Child completion become long compliance-shaped streams with repeated records and unclear handoffs. The design system itself is disciplined; the main problem is composition, lifecycle-aware UX, and information hierarchy.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

Seven of eight cognitive-load checks fail. Grouping passes, but single focus, chunking, hierarchy, one-thing-at-a-time, minimal choice, working-memory relief, and progressive disclosure fail in the task flow. The opening feels confident and warm; task drafting becomes a prerequisite maze; review and Child task feel bureaucratic; the praise/garden consequence is the emotional peak; repeated empty landscape records and capability disclosures dilute the ending.

## Priority findings

### P1 — Recognition does not fail closed on stale or forged references

`applyRecognition` trusts a caller-supplied recognition key and does not prove that assignment, task/Child/version, submission, check-in, plan, and active journey belong together. A corrupted `taskVersion = 99` and forged key still mutated Seeds, Mangrove progress, canopy, and circle. Derive the key internally and validate the full reference chain immediately before projection/mutation.

### P1 — Accepted alternatives can be internally invalid yet remain executable

Accepting the smaller/safe-equivalent proposal retains the original task/template identity while replacing content and losing Parent wording. The resulting task fails Ghaf's own validator, but later transitions can still execute and recognize it. Model a coherent replacement version, preserve Parent authorship/provenance, update linked versions atomically, and guard every downstream transition.

### P1 — Guide suggestion acceptance has no resolved UI state

While a suggestion is pending, Parent text, Guide intents, and Review remain active. Accepting the suggestion leaves the same Accept/Keep panel visible and provides no confirmation. Editing before acceptance can silently clear the suggestion and produce a generic error. Treat the comparison as an explicit decision state; block conflicting controls and clear the pending suggestion after acceptance while showing a concise applied record.

### P1 — The active Child task is buried and lifecycle actions become false

Two preview-only future cards render before the sole executable task. Back/re-entry and recognized/submitted states expose Choose or Ask Smaller actions that services reject, and recognized work can produce the unrelated “Future option” error. Put active work first, separate compact previews, and derive actions/routes from lifecycle: choose, start, resume, awaiting Parent, or see what grew.

### P1 — Parent drafting and review overwhelm despite good safeguards

Before Salem is selected, eight category chips look active even though they are disabled. Later steps remain visible before they are actionable. The uncollapsed review correctly preserves all required bilingual safety content, but recognition/award/phase/recurrence/landscape are then repeated in a second metadata ledger. Visibly sequence Child → category → template → refine, and remove repeated policy records without hiding required fields.

### P1 — Child task reads like an adult compliance document

The first viewport is consumed by a long definition. Four static steps, four Coach intents, a separate adult exit, repeated Coach steps, two full media records, reflection, acknowledgement, visibility copy, and Submit follow in one stream. Preserve the approved definition and safety facts, but provide a short child-facing summary first, make Coach output intent-specific, reduce simultaneous choices, and progressively reveal optional evidence.

### P1 — Cross-role handoffs omit success and the next actor

Assignment approval returns to an unchanged role selector, with no “assigned to Salem” state or dominant continuation. Parent home can also render View garden as both primary and secondary. Add journey-aware handoff copy/action and suppress duplicate navigation.

### P2 — The garden climax becomes an equal-weight inventory

All five tracks render as full records, pushing canopy and circle several screens down. Keep Mangrove as the changed hero and render the four unchanged required tracks as compact connected markers.

### P2 — RTL accent and repeated disclosure/copy details weaken polish

The garden disclosure's physical-left 4px accent is directionally inconsistent in Arabic. Role profile accessible names repeat “synthetic,” the role disclosure repeats its subtitle, Parent canopy meaning is duplicated, and `TaskSteps` contains a redundant nested wrapper. Correct these small but visible craft defects.

### P2 — Optional provider timeout is simulated, not enforced

The store awaits a primary refinement indefinitely and only handles a returned TIMEOUT error. Enforce the documented 1500 ms deadline and ignore late completion before any optional adapter can be enabled.
